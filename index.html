Chih-Hao (Justin) Ku, Ph.D.

Associate Professor · Information Technology and Decision Sciences
G. Brint Ryan College of Business, University of North Texas

I study how natural language processing, large language models and visual analytics can turn large volumes of text, such as reports, reviews and social media, into knowledge decision makers can act on. My work has appeared in the Journal of Management Information Systems, Information & Management, Tourism Management, JASIST, Government Information Quarterly and the Journal of Business Research, among others.

I am an Associate Editor of the Journal of Computer Information Systems. I received my M.S. and Ph.D. in Information Systems and Technology from Claremont Graduate University and taught at Cleveland State University before joining UNT in 2023. Research collaboration is welcome.

Research

Text analytics and visual analytics for decision support, applied to public safety, hospitality and tourism, and online platforms.

Crime report analysisEntity extraction, document similarity and visualization to help analysts link related crimes.
Online reviews and response strategyDeep learning and linguistic features to identify the critical reviews firms should answer first.
Deep learning and tourismVisual analytics and convolutional neural networks on 113,685 TripAdvisor reviews and managerial responses.
COVID-19 and the airline industryAspect-based sentiment analysis with BERT on airline reviews before and during the pandemic.
Social media analyticsSentiment analysis, aspect extraction and deep learning on user-generated content in hospitality.
Crowdsourcing analysisTopic modeling and sentiment analysis of crowdsourcing tasks on Amazon Mechanical Turk and of executives' social media.
